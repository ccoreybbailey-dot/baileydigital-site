Privacy

Updated 21 November 2026

This site does two things with information: it sends me what you write in the form, and it counts visits.

The form

The form asks for your name, your email, how many people are on your team, and your answers to two questions about how your company works. When you send it, a form service called Web3Forms passes it on to my email, which is with Zoho Mail in their Canadian data centre. I use what you send only to reply to you and to talk about the work. If we don't end up working together, I delete it within a year of when you sent it.

Counting visits

Visits are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. It records which pages were seen, where visitors came from (a link on LinkedIn, a search), and general details like the browser, the kind of device and the country. It doesn't record who you are.
