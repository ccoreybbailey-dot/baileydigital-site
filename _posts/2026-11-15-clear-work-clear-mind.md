I’m a trades guy who builds tools for clear work and a clear mind.

My first build was [ChantierLog](/#chantierlog), a job reporting app that keeps the story of the day clear and organized so it can be sent off to the office or the boss for billing. The concept was simple: replace the paper sheets we were already filling out every day with a simple, easy-to-use form on your phone.

But there is another side to me, one I don’t talk about at work. I am a deeply reflective person. I read and think a lot about philosophy, different religious teachings, and psychology — how people actually think. It’s something I have always been curious about, and it probably takes up most of my thinking on any given day. That can be both a blessing and a curse. Getting overwhelmed and losing clarity can be one of the biggest problems.

As a reflective person, I journal regularly. Recently I added voice to my journaling routine. Almost every morning, on my walk with my dog before work, I would open voice memos or the video recorder and just brain dump. Those brain dumps could include anything: my struggles, my insights, direction, goals.

When you record every day, you end up with a lot of recordings, and sifting through them becomes a chore in itself. On top of that, I had been struggling with short-form video, reels to be specific. That’s where the idea for [Second Pass](https://secondpass.app) came from: a reflective app that interrupts you at random with your own brain dumps and insights. The idea of being interrupted at random came from a night I was mindlessly scrolling through reels, and one came up telling me to go do something with my life. It snapped me out of my trance.

At first glance the two products seem wildly different. But look a little closer and you can see the same pattern in both: capture it in the moment so it doesn’t get lost, and make sense of it later.

Clear work, clear mind — that’s the thread. Two tools, one point of view. Practical tools, built by someone who understands how people work and think, because I have lived both sides.

I’m going to write here about both. About building tools for trades companies — simple systems, AI features, custom products that a tired human will actually use. And about the reflective side, the thinking that makes the work clearer in the first place.

If either of those sounds like you, stick around.
