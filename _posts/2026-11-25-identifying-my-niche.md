In the age of AI, it has never been easier to start an online business. The trouble is not starting the business — it’s having a clear, focused vision of what you want the business to be. I have been struggling with that for the last seven months, since I started using AI tools back in April 2026.

Coming from a background in the trades, there are times I doubt my abilities. I build things and run heavy equipment; how does that transfer to developing software? I have many interests and don’t always know where to start. With so many interests, I tend to jump around, trying to figure out how to put it all together.

Logically, sticking with building tools for the trades makes sense. I know the business, I understand the people, and I’ve seen the problems first hand for years. So starting there made sense. ChantierLog, a job reporting app, was something I could design from my own experience. It is a good product that I put a lot of thought into. I just question the passion behind it. I mean, I do get some joy thinking over a problem and trying to find a solution that helps.

But digging a little deeper, one problem I have always faced, and wanted to find solutions for, is understanding my thoughts and the inner workings of the mind. I have always been into self-improvement, and I’ve grown a lot through inner work. Like many people these days, I am a victim of distraction. My pull towards distraction really comes from an inability to silence the voice in my head. It becomes much easier to distract myself than to sit and observe.

Journaling and voice recordings, aimed at getting thoughts out of my head, have helped a great deal with this. Second Pass was my attempt to use those methods to quiet the mind and clarify my thoughts.

These are two very different aims and products. So I’ve been thinking about how I can merge these two interests into something that is its own niche, or brand.

Looking for the pattern, I saw that ChantierLog and Second Pass are both about organization and focus. ChantierLog aims to tell the story of the day, and Second Pass aims to tell the story in your head.

Finding solutions to keeping jobs organized and thoughts organized is where my attention seems to be drawn. So those are the kinds of products and systems I am looking to build for owners and team members alike.
